*A voice for the change we haven't made yet.*

## What we work on

| Pillar | Campaign | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Elections** | **2020, 20Twenty & Beyond** | "Vote for vision, vote for reason." Helps young Americans learn about local and federal candidates and their ideas, through the website, social media, and community events. |
| **Environment** | **SAVE** (Sun, Air, Vapor, Earth) | Promotes everyday habits and green practices that keep the planet livable. |
| **Equality** | **WAR** (Who Are Result) | A campaign against hate and racism that promotes respect and equality regardless of race, ethnicity, or background. |

## What's on the site

A single page with these sections:

- **Home:** the mission and an introduction
- **Projects:** each campaign explained
- **Blog:** writing on voting and civic life
- **About:** the community of young organizers behind it
- **Contact:** email, and a mailing list you can join
- **Kart Production:** a link to the parent studio
