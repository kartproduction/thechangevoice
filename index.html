The Change Voice runs four youth-driven projects, each built around one thing worth raising your voice for: who we vote for, the planet we live on, and the people we owe respect.

Four projects, one mission

Independent efforts, run by young organizers, aimed at the same goal — a more informed, more sustainable, more equal country.

Elections

2020, 20Twenty & Beyond

Vote for vision, vote for reason

Empowering American youth to take part in local and federal elections by getting to know the candidates and their ideas. We believe informed voting is crucial to a healthy democracy and that every vote counts — so through our website, social media channels, and community events, we educate and mobilize young voters across the country.

Environment

SAVE

Sun · Air · Vapor · Earth

A campaign for environmental awareness — promoting the everyday habits and green practices that keep the planet livable for the generations coming after us.

Equality

WAR

Who Are Result — a message against hate

A platform standing against hate and racism. WAR holds that everyone deserves respect and equality regardless of race, ethnicity, or background, and works to spread that message everywhere it can.

About us

The Change Voice is a community of young organizers who believe that real change starts with people willing to speak up — at the ballot box, in their neighborhoods, and against hate wherever they find it. Join us on social media to follow what each project is doing next.
