The Importance of Voting in a Democratic Society

Khada N. Acharya · Jan 1, 2019 · 1 min read

Not Voting equal to giving your Voice

All of the elections are decided by "We the people", who go out and vote for someone who has similar agendas. You should take some time and learn about the candidates who are running for the office, even if it is local elections. If you do not vote, someone will vote and make the decision for you. Not only that, agendas you are fighting for will be in jeopardy. Your power is in your vote, so vote.

It's your money, not politicians

We all pay taxes. Yes, you all pay taxes but do not know how your taxes money is being used? Most of you have no ideas where the taxes money is going? By voting, you have the chance to know how your taxes money is being used, such as funding for healthcare, education and so on.

Vote is equal to an Opportunity for Change

If you all want to make a good impact, voting gives you all the chance to make a good impact. Vote the candidates that help your community, state and the nation for the good of "We the people". Make your voice heard by voting. Let's vote for every election. Let's VOTE.
